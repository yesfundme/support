Responsible Fundraising

### Our Commitment to Verified Fundraising **YesFundMe is committed to making fundraising safer, more transparent, and more trustworthy for everyone.** We take the legitimacy of every fundraising campaign seriously and work to ensure that campaigns created on our platform are supported by genuine information, appropriate documentation, and evidence that helps verify the purpose of the fundraiser. Before a fundraising campaign is approved, YesFundMe may require organizers to provide **official documents, supporting records, identification of the fundraising purpose, and other relevant proof** depending on the nature of the campaign. This verification process is designed to help confirm that the person or organization requesting support is genuine and that the funds are being raised for the purpose stated in the campaign. We believe donors deserve to know where their contributions are going. That is why we encourage transparency throughout the fundraising process, including clear explanations of the situation, the intended use of funds, and supporting evidence where appropriate. Our goal is to reduce fraudulent or misleading campaigns and create an environment where donors can contribute with greater confidence. **Every fundraiser matters, and so does every donation.** YesFundMe works to maintain standards that protect both fundraisers and donors by reviewing available information and documentation before campaigns are accepted. Where additional verification is necessary, we may request further evidence to establish the authenticity of a campaign. Our verification efforts are part of our broader commitment to **responsible fundraising, accountability, and transparency**. While no verification system can guarantee that every claim made by an organizer is true, YesFundMe takes reasonable steps to review submitted information and identify campaigns that appear suspicious, misleading, or unsupported. By promoting verified and properly documented fundraising, YesFundMe aims to connect people who genuinely need assistance with people who are willing to help. **Your trust is important to us, and we continuously work to make fundraising more secure, credible, and transparent.**
